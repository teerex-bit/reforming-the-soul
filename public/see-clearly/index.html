FROM AWARENESS TO CLARITY

Noticing is only the beginning.

We do not respond only to what happens. We respond to what we believe it means. Our assumptions, expectations, wounds, and formed ways of seeing can make something feel true without making it true.

See Clearly slows the moment down long enough to ask: What am I seeing? What am I assuming? Is it true?

Clarity precedes obedience.

A FOUNDATIONAL REALITY

You are not trying to become alive.

In Christ, you already are a new creation. The old person was crucified with Christ. What remains are formed patterns, beliefs, habits, and ways of seeing that must be brought into the light.

“If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old things have passed away. Behold, all things have become new.”2 Corinthians 5:17
