See Who You
Really Are

Awaken helped you notice what is happening. Here, you learn to understand what you see—and test it against what is true.

In Christ, you already are a new creation. The old person was crucified with Christ; what remains are formed patterns, beliefs, habits, and ways of seeing.

You are not trying to become alive. You are learning to make room for the Life already within you.

Two Movements. One Journey.

01
Part 1

See Yourself Clearly

Examine the stories, assumptions, and patterns behind what you see, and test them against your identity in Christ.

→
02
Part 2

See God Clearly

Look again through Jesus and receive the truer reality of God’s love and goodness.

The goal of spiritual formation is not to become something we are not, but to become more fully who we already are in Christ.

Key Truth

If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!— 2 Corinthians 5:17

Foundational Realities

  • ◉You are a new creation. The old person died with Christ.
  • ⌕God is good, loving, and committed to your good.

In This Phase You Will

  • ◉Learn to recognize the difference between what you see and what is true.
  • ⌕Examine your beliefs, assumptions, and inherited perspectives.
  • ♥Discover God’s heart for you as revealed through Jesus.
  • ❧Begin to see life through the lens of the Kingdom.

Remember

Clarity is not about having all the answers. It’s about being willing to look again with God.
