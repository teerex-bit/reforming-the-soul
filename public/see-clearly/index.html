Clearly.

We do not respond only to what happens. We respond to what we believe it means.

LEARNING TO LOOK AGAIN

What feels true is not always true.

Something can feel completely true because it has been reinforced by years of experience. But familiarity is not the same as truth. Seeing clearly creates space between what we feel and what we choose to believe.

Seeing clearly means slowing down long enough to ask: What am I seeing? What am I assuming? Is it true?

This happens in two connected parts: learning to see yourself clearly and learning to see God clearly.

TWO PARTS · ONE CLEARER VIEW

Where will you look?

Begin by examining the way you see yourself. Then look at the picture of God you carry and test it against what Jesus reveals.
