BRINGING IT TOGETHER

What you see shapes how you live.

“The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore when your eye is good, your whole body is also full of light.”

Luke 11:34

Before moving forward, pause and notice what has come into view. Seeing clearly is not merely having better information. It changes the place from which you trust, choose, and live.

WHAT HAS COME INTO VIEW

A clearer view changes what becomes possible.

01

What feels true is not always true.*

Familiar thoughts and reactions may have been reinforced for years. Their familiarity gives them influence, but it does not make them accurate.

02

Formation is not identity.*

Your stories, wounds, assumptions, and learned patterns affect the way you see, but they do not define who you are in Christ.

03

Your picture of God shapes your response to Him.*

When Jesus corrects distorted expectations of God, trust can begin to replace fear, performance, distance, and control.

04

Clarity creates room for a different choice.*

When you can recognize what is shaping you, you are no longer required to respond from habit. You can begin choosing from what is true.

* Explored more fully in the deeper-dive curriculum.

FROM SEEING TO LIVING

Change begins deeper than behavior.

What you see
shapes what you believe.

What you believe
shapes what you expect.

What you expect
influences what you desire.

What you desire
influences what you intend.

What you intend
shapes what you choose.

What you repeatedly choose
becomes how you live.

THE NEXT MOVEMENT

Now we begin to Become.

Seeing clearly changes the place from which you live. Become carries that clearer vision into the will, body, relationships, soul, and the fruit of everyday life. This is the longer work of learning to live with God as the person you already are in Christ.
