A DOORWAY INTO THE JOURNEY

You did not become
who you are
all at once.

Long before you could explain what was happening,
you were being formed.

What got attention.

Who could be trusted.

What failure meant.

What God was like.

That is formation.

WHAT WE CARRY FORWARD

Those lessons did not stay in the past.

They became expectations, reactions, desires, defenses, habits, and ways of seeing.

The patterns you carry have a history. They do not have to have the final word.

Jesus does more than ask us to behave differently. He meets us in the places where we have been formed and teaches you another way to see, trust, desire, and live.

That is where Reforming the Soul begins.

A SIMPLE DEFINITION

Formation is the process by which we become the kind of people we are.

Over time, our experiences, relationships, and beliefs shape how we see, what we expect, what we desire, what we fear, what we protect, and how we respond.

Some of that formation has helped us grow. Other parts have taught us patterns that may once have protected us but now keep us from responding freely to what God is doing.

The question is not whether you have been formed, but what has formed you.

What has been forming you -
and what kind of person are you becoming?

Reforming the Soul begins by noticing what has shaped us, seeing it clearly, and becoming available to God's transforming work.

LOOK BENEATH BEHAVIOR

Behavior comes from somewhere deeper.

We usually notice problems at the level of behavior: worry, anger, impatience, control, relationship patterns, or difficulty trusting God.

But behavior comes from somewhere deeper.

  1. What I see→shapes what I believe.
  2. What I believe→shapes what I expect.
  3. What I expect→influences what I desire.
  4. What I desire→influences what I intend.
  5. What I intend→shapes what I choose.
  6. What I repeatedly choose→becomes how I live.

Reforming the Soul goes beneath behavior. The goal is not merely to manage what we do, but to allow God to reform the person from whom those behaviors come.

“Be transformed by the renewing of your mind…”

Romans 12:2

THE FORMATION JOURNEY

A direction for the journey.

The four movements give formation a way forward.

Awaken

Notice what is happening.

Pay attention to your reactions, patterns, and desires before trying to change them.

See Clearly

Test what feels true.

See yourself clearly, see God through Jesus, and understand how what you see shapes how you live.

Become

Bring truth into the whole person.

Learn to live with God through your will, body, relationships, soul, and the fruit of everyday life.

Join

Participate in what God is doing.

Offer the person you are becoming to God’s work in the people and places around you.
