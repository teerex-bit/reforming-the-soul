THE JOURNEY BECOMES A LIFE

God restored your soul to be useful.

Usefulness is not the same as busyness, importance, or religious visibility. It is the growing freedom to become a faithful presence through whom God can love, serve, reconcile, encourage, and restore.

THE FORMATION JOURNEY

A formed life becomes a shared gift.

  1. 01Notice honestly.

    Pay attention to what is happening within and around you.

  2. 02See truthfully.

    Test what feels true against who God is and who you are in Christ.

  3. 03Live faithfully.

    Let truth take shape in your choices, body, relationships, and soul.

  4. 04Become available.

    Offer your formed life to the good work God has already begun.

EPHESIANS 2:10

“For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared before that we would walk in them.”
Ephesians 2:10

RETURN TO THE ORDINARY

The work is already near you.

Participation returns you to ordinary relationships and responsibilities with greater attention and availability. You do not need to become someone else to join God’s work; you can offer the life He is forming in you, right where you are.
