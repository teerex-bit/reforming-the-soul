MOVEMENT FOUR

A restored life becomes available.

God does not restore your life only for your own relief. The person He is forming becomes increasingly free to notice what He is doing and available to take part in it.

A basic shape for joining in.

This overview gives you the basic shape of Join. The deeper-dive curriculum takes more time to explore how discernment, availability, service, and shared life become faithful participation.

A LIFE OFFERED IN ORDINARY PLACES

Participation starts with attention.

01

Notice where God is already at work.*

You do not have to invent a purpose large enough to matter. Begin with the people, needs, responsibilities, and interruptions already near you. Join starts by paying attention to the work God has begun before you arrived.

02

Offer the person you are becoming.*

Love, peace, patience, courage, freedom, and availability are not private accomplishments. They become ways God can bring His life into the ordinary places where you are already present.

* Explored more fully in the deeper-dive curriculum.

RESTORED FOR GOOD WORK

Participation begins close to home.

Joining God’s work rarely begins with a grand assignment. It grows through the people you know, the responsibilities you carry, and the faithful next response that makes room for love.
