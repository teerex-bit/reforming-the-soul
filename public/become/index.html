MOVEMENT THREE

Truth must become a way of living.

Seeing clearly matters because it changes the place from which you live. Become is where truth begins to take shape in how you choose, respond, relate, and remain with God.

A basic view of a deeper work.

This overview gives you the basic shape of Become without unpacking every practice or area of formation. A more comprehensive curriculum is available for those who want to explore each part more deeply and work through it over time.

TWO PARTS · ONE PERSON

Formation becomes embodied.

Become moves in two connected directions. First, you learn a repeatable way of living with God in the actual moments of your life. Then you begin to see how that life reaches into every dimension of the person.

01

Live With God

Practice presence, listening, and surrender through a simple rhythm you can return to in any moment.

02

The Whole Person

Allow life with God to reach the will, body, relationships, soul, and the fruit your life produces.

BEGIN WITH PART ONE

Learn to live with God here.

Transformation does not happen somewhere outside your real life. It begins as you meet God in the moment already in front of you.
