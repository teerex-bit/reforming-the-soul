PART TWO · THE WHOLE PERSON

God forms
more than
your thoughts.

Transformation reaches every dimension of human life. God is not improving one religious corner of you. He is bringing the whole person into an increasingly integrated life with Him.

WHOLE
  1. Will
  2. Body
  3. Relationships
  4. Soul

ONE LIFE · FOUR DIMENSIONS

Each part affects
the others.

The will chooses, the body carries practiced responses, relationships reveal what is being formed, and the soul holds the person together. Formation becomes durable as these dimensions learn a shared direction under God.

01

The Will*

The will is your capacity to intend and choose. A formed will is not merely strong; it is increasingly settled toward what is good, even when another response remains available.

02

The Body*

The body remembers hurry, fear, tension, avoidance, and appetite. It can also learn stillness, restraint, courage, rest, and faithful presence.

03

Relationships*

Formation becomes visible between people. Love is practiced through truthfulness, listening, boundaries, forgiveness, patience, and remaining present without control.

04

The Soul*

The soul integrates the person. Wholeness grows as mind, will, body, and relationships stop pulling in separate directions and become ordered around life with God.

* Explored more fully in the deeper-dive curriculum.

WHOLENESS

Integration is not perfection.

A whole person is not someone who never struggles. It is someone whose life is becoming less divided—someone increasingly able to bring thought, desire, action, and relationship into the same faithful response.

The goal is not to manage separate parts of yourself. It is to become the kind of person whose whole life is available to God.

WHAT BECOMES VISIBLE

A tree is known by its fruit.

The work happening within the person eventually becomes recognizable in the life that person produces.
