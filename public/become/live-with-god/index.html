PART ONE · LIVE WITH GOD

Formation happens in
the life you are already
living.

Life with God is not reserved for unusually spiritual moments. It becomes real as you learn to notice His presence, listen for His direction, and surrender your need to control what happens next.

The moment in front of you becomes the place where trust is practiced.

A POSTURE BEFORE A TECHNIQUE

Presence. Listening. Surrender.

Presence keeps you available to what God is doing now. Listening makes room for His wisdom rather than your first impulse. Surrender releases the demand that the moment unfold according to your plan.

01

Presence*

Return your attention to God and to the reality of the moment instead of disappearing into fear, memory, or imagined outcomes.

02

Listening*

Become quiet enough to recognize what is true and what faithful love may require here.

03

Surrender*

Release the outcome and become willing to follow God through the next faithful choice.

04

Practice*

Return to the pattern often enough that availability to God becomes increasingly natural.

* Explored more fully in the deeper-dive curriculum.

A REPEATABLE RHYTHM

A way to meet any moment.

The rhythm is simple enough to remember and deep enough to practice for a lifetime. It does not control the moment. It helps you become available to God within it.

01

Release control

Let go of the demand to manage the moment, the people in it, or the outcome.

02

Receive the moment

Accept that this is the moment in which God is present and able to form you.

03

Take the next right step

Choose the next faithful response instead of trying to solve the whole future.

04

Repeat

Return to the rhythm whenever control, fear, hurry, or habit begins leading again.

PAUSE AND NOTICE

Practice is forming someone.

Repeated choices do more than change what you do. They train the person from whom your actions come.
