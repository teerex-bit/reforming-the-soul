# Become Overview Design

## Purpose

Build a five-page overview of Become that gives readers a basic understanding of the movement without reproducing the deeper curriculum. The introduction explains that a comprehensive curriculum is available for readers who want to explore and practice each part in detail.

## Sequence

1. **Become — Introduction** at `/become/`
   - Seeing clearly is not the destination; truth must enter daily life.
   - Introduces two parts: Live With God and The Whole Person.
   - Includes one concise overview/deeper-dive note.
   - Leads to Part 1.

2. **Part 1 — Live With God** at `/become/live-with-god/`
   - Presence, listening, and surrender.
   - Daily rhythm: Release control → Receive the moment → Take the next right step → Repeat.
   - Leads to the pause page.

3. **Pause — Practice Forms the Person** at `/become/practice-forms-the-person/`
   - Repeated choices form more than behavior.
   - Bridges daily practice into the will, body, relationships, and soul.
   - Leads to Part 2.

4. **Part 2 — The Whole Person** at `/become/whole-person/`
   - The will: intention and choice.
   - The body: trained reactions and habits.
   - Relationships: giving and receiving love.
   - The soul: integration of the whole person under God.
   - Leads to Fruit.

5. **Fruit — What a Changed Person Looks Like** at `/become/fruit/`
   - Transformation becomes visible as love, peace, patience, courage, freedom, and availability to God.
   - Completes Become and leads to Join.

## Design

Use the existing Overview editorial system: Tree of Life logo, navy/paper/green/gold palette, live HTML text, Georgia display type, restrained imagery, responsive two-column sections, and a navy closing transition on each page. Keep each page to approximately one or two screens of essential content.

## Content boundaries

- Do not duplicate See Clearly.
- Do not reproduce the full 15–16 page curriculum.
- Mention the deeper curriculum once on the Become introduction.
- Keep all progression sequential; content cards explain structure but do not function as a menu.
- Do not create or link Join until a valid Join page exists.
- The final Fruit page may state that Join comes next without linking to a missing route.

## Doorway

Update `public/index.html` to list all five Become pages after the existing See Clearly pages.
